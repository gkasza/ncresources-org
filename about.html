What this is

NC Resources is a free, mobile-first directory of community help — food banks, shelters, rent and utility assistance, legal aid, mental health crisis support, childcare and WIC, diaper banks, transportation, healthcare, free tax prep, and more — covering all 100 North Carolina counties. It exists to help families in need (and the social workers supporting them) quickly find a real address, real hours, and a real phone number.

It is built and maintained by a family in Durham whose loved one works in social services — they kept seeing referrals delayed because existing directories were stale or hard to use on a phone, so they built this. No organization, no donations requested, no ads, no tracking, no login. Use what's helpful and share it freely.

Important: Information on this site was gathered from each agency's own website, official NC DHHS county directories, food-bank partner networks, and public referral directories. Agency hours, phone numbers, and funding change frequently. Please call before you go.

Where the data comes from

  • Each agency's own website & official portals — we cross-check agency sites, official NC DHHS county Department of Social Services (DSS) directories, Legal Aid of NC, and regional food bank hubs to confirm hours, phones, addresses, and eligibility.
  • Public referral directories and partner networks — county human-services pages, Food Bank of Central & Eastern NC, MANNA FoodBank, Second Harvest Metrolina & Northwest NC, Food Bank of the Albemarle, Inter-Faith Food Shuttle, Diaper Bank of NC, Smart Start, and 2-1-1 aggregators.
  • Conflict policy — when sources disagree, the agency's own site wins, and the "Last checked" date on each listing lets you gauge freshness.

How to read each listing

  • Hours — program-specific hours when known (food pantry hours rather than general office hours).
  • Who — eligibility (county residency, age, income, or referral requirements).
  • Bring — documents the agency asks for (photo ID, proof of address, utility bill, etc.).
  • How — how to apply (call, walk in, register online, or by appointment).
  • Share / Print — every card has a 1-tap Share/Copy button so social workers can text details directly to a client, and every filtered view prints cleanly without page clutter.

What this directory is NOT

  • It is not an emergency service. For emergencies dial 911.
  • It is not a 24/7 helpline. For 24/7 referrals dial 2-1-1; for mental health crisis call or text 988.
  • It is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or operated by any of the agencies listed here.
  • It is not a substitute for calling the agency to confirm availability before traveling.

Privacy

This site does not require an account. It does not log searches. It does not use tracking cookies, ads, or third-party analytics scripts. Your calls, map directions, and shared links go straight through your device.
