What this is

NC Resources is a free, mobile-first directory of community help — food banks, shelters, rent and utility assistance, mental health crisis support, childcare and WIC, dental and medical clinics, diaper banks, transportation, free tax prep, and more — covering all 100 North Carolina counties. It exists to help families in need (and the social workers supporting them) quickly find a real address, real hours, and a real phone number.

It is built and maintained by a family in Durham whose loved one works in social services — they kept seeing referrals delayed because existing directories were stale or hard to use on a phone, so they built this. No organization, no donations requested, no ads, no tracking, no login. Use what's helpful and share it freely.

Important: Information on this site was gathered from each agency's own website, official NC DHHS county directories, food-bank partner networks, and public referral directories. Agency hours, phone numbers, and funding change frequently. Please call before you go.

Where the data comes from

  • Each agency's own website & official portals — we cross-check agency sites, official NC DHHS county Department of Social Services (DSS) and public health directories, and regional food bank hubs to confirm hours, phones, addresses, and eligibility.
  • Public referral directories and partner networks — county human-services pages, Food Bank of Central & Eastern NC, MANNA FoodBank, Second Harvest Metrolina & Northwest NC, Food Bank of the Albemarle, Inter-Faith Food Shuttle, Diaper Bank of NC, Smart Start, ECU/UNC dental clinics, and 2-1-1 aggregators.
  • Honest verification dates — each listing's "Last checked" date reflects when that specific entry was verified. If an agency's website is unreachable, we flag it with a caution banner rather than hiding the problem.

Helpful tools built in

  • Save a custom shortlist — tap the bookmark icon on any listings to build a personal shortlist on your device, then print or copy the entire shortlist to text to a client or family member.
  • Share in 1 tap — every card has a Share button that formats the agency's name, address, phone, and hours cleanly for SMS or messaging apps.
  • Print-ready handouts — tap "Print list" from any filtered view or saved shortlist to print a clean handout with no page clutter.
  • Social Security card / SSN indicator — programs that ask for a Social Security card or number are highlighted in red with a checkmark in "Who can apply", and you can filter them in or out from the results bar.
  • English & Español — switch the interface between English and Spanish at any time from the top navigation bar.

What this directory is NOT

  • It is not an emergency service. For emergencies dial 911.
  • It is not a 24/7 helpline. For 24/7 referrals dial 2-1-1; for mental health crisis call or text 988.
  • It is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or operated by any of the agencies listed here.
  • It is not a substitute for calling the agency to confirm availability before traveling.

Privacy

This site does not require an account. It does not log searches. It does not use tracking cookies, ads, or third-party analytics scripts. Any saved shortlist stays strictly in your own browser's local storage and never leaves your device.
